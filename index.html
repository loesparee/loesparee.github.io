About me

Hi! I'm Loes — a calm, reliable hard worker with a technical mindset, currently doing my MSc in Geo-Information Science at Wageningen University & Research.

My interest in GIS took me from a bachelor's in Animal Management into the world of geospatial data. During my master's I've picked up a lot of new skills in the GIS/RS field. I love puzzling with tools and data to turn them into results people can actually see and understand.

Outside my studies you'll usually find me horseback riding, hiking, watching films, or doing puzzles.
